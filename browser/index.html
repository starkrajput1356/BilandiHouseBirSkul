Birla Open Minds International School, Hoshiarpur

Nurturing India’s Tomorrow. A Yash Birla Group K-12 school on Tanda Road, Hoshiarpur, offering Pre-Nursery to High School with joyful, experiential, child-centred learning. Admissions open for 2027–28.

Part of the Birla Open Minds network founded by the Yash Birla Group, our Hoshiarpur campus brings Pre-Nursery to Grade XII together on one green, secure campus. Children learn through hands-on STEM labs, a digital library, robotics, arts, music, drama and sport, guided by caring and qualified teachers. Our indoor arena, play zones, basketball and cricket academy build strong bodies alongside curious minds, and our experiential, child-centred curriculum moves every learner from concrete understanding to independent, confident thinking.
